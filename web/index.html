Offline Android operations for local records, container tracking, equipment checkout, and revenue review.

Daily operations in one local workspace

The app is built for staff and volunteers who need a clear phone workflow without accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or outside services.

Patient records Keep local follow-up notes, status, and next actions ready for the day.
Containers Monitor location, route, capacity, and review status before events.
Equipment Track checkouts, due dates, and return status from the field.
Revenue Review room hire, memberships, equipment fees, and event takings.

Designed for repeat use

Bottom navigation keeps the main work areas close. Forms save records locally, status controls update asset workflows, and privacy notes stay visible in settings.

  • No Android permissions declared by the app.
  • No external APIs, analytics, login, or cloud sync.
  • Records remain on the device until app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled.
